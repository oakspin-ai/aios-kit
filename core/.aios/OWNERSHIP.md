# Who owns what

Plain-English statement of who owns what during the design-partner phase. Approved by OakSpin's
owner on 2026-11-03. A licence document replaces this before the first paid install.

| Part | Owner | If we part ways |
|---|---|---|
| Everything about your business: `profile.md`, `rules.md`, `knowledge/`, `templates/`, `feedback.md`, `company/`, `clients/`, `memory/`, `inbox/`, `skills/` written for you | You | Keeps working. Take it anywhere. |
| The engine: `.aios/` (interview, playbooks, tools, templates, hooks, your industry pack's checks) | OakSpin AI, licensed to you | The installed version keeps working with your own AI account. Updates stop. |
| Tuning, support, new capabilities, hosting of anything always-on | OakSpin AI, as a paid service | Stops. |
| Your AI account and connectors | You, with the provider | Unchanged. |

Nothing in this brain phones home to OakSpin. OakSpin sees your brain only when you invite an
OakSpin account into your repository, and you can remove it any time.
